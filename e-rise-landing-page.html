About Our School

Why families choose us

English + Programming, together

Our teachers spent eight years teaching English conversation at Eishunjuku, a prep school in Makino. To offer an even more advanced English education, we opened e-Rise in Kuzuha — combining the strengths of English-conversation teaching and exam-prep methodology for your child.

  • ✓Phonics, for preschoolers through junior high schoolers
  • ✓Speaking & Listening built around real conversation
  • ✓Reading & Writing with a strong grammar foundation
  • ✓Eiken® test preparation, Grades 5 through 2
